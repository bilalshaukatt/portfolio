Our story

A small shop with a simple promise

Third Street Coffee opened in 2014 with one espresso machine and a handful of regulars. A decade later, the machine's bigger but the promise hasn't changed: good beans, treated with care, served by people who know your order.

Everything on our menu is made to order, no batching, no shortcuts. If it's not fresh, we don't serve it.
